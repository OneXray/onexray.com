# OneXray

> Configuration and VPN Tunnel guide for people and AI assistants. OneXray is a client; it supplies no servers or credentials.

Read the complete guide before producing configuration. Follow the documented field and platform constraints without silently dropping conditions. Choose outbound, Custom Routing, or Raw JSON before generating a document. VPN Tunnel settings are separate UI operations, not importable JSON fields. Never invent credentials or claim an untested template is a working VPN.
